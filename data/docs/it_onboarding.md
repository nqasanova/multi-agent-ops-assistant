# IT Onboarding for New Employees

## First day
New employees receive a laptop with the standard software image, a company account and access to email and chat on their first day. Hardware requests beyond the standard laptop go through the IT service desk.

## Accounts and access
Access to systems beyond email and chat is requested by the team lead through the IT service desk and is usually granted within 2 business days. Access to production systems also needs approval from the platform engineering lead.

## Security training
Every new employee must complete the security awareness training within their first 14 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.
