# Data Access Policy

## Principle of least privilege
Employees get access only to the systems and data they need for their current role. Access is reviewed every quarter by the team lead and revoked within 24 hours when someone changes role or leaves the company.

## Customer data
Customer personal data may only be exported from the CRM for a documented business purpose. Exports containing personal data must be stored on encrypted company drives and deleted after 30 days.

## AI tools and customer data
Customer personal data must not be pasted into external AI tools unless the tool is on the approved vendor list and a data processing agreement is in place. Internal AI assistants may query the operational database only through read-only access.

## Production databases
Direct write access to production databases is limited to the platform engineering team. All other teams use read-only replicas.
