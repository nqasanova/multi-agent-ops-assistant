# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day ship the same day. Standard delivery within Germany takes 2 to 3 business days, delivery to other EU countries takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free for orders above 250 EUR within the EU. Orders below that amount are charged a flat 9.90 EUR.

## Express delivery
Express delivery within Germany arrives the next business day and costs 24.90 EUR. Enterprise customers get express delivery at no extra cost.

## Lost shipments
If tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, the shipment is treated as lost and a replacement is sent free of charge.
