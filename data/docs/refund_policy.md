# Refund and Credit Policy

## Eligibility
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date if the goods are unused and in their original packaging. After 30 days and up to 90 days, we offer store credit only, minus a 15% restocking fee.

## Damaged or incorrect deliveries
If an order arrives damaged or does not match the order confirmation, the customer is entitled to a full refund or free replacement regardless of the 30 day window, as long as the issue is reported within 14 days of delivery with photos attached to the support ticket.

## Approval limits
Support agents can approve refunds up to 500 EUR on their own. Refunds between 500 EUR and 5,000 EUR need approval from a team lead. Anything above 5,000 EUR must be approved by the finance department.

## Enterprise customers
Enterprise tier customers have a 60 day full refund window instead of 30 days, as agreed in their master service agreement.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
