# Customer Support Service Levels

## First response times
First response targets depend on the customer's tier and the ticket priority.

For Enterprise customers, the first response target is 1 hour for high priority tickets, 4 hours for medium priority tickets and 1 business day for low priority tickets.

For Business customers, the first response target is 4 hours for high priority tickets, 1 business day for medium priority tickets and 2 business days for low priority tickets.

For Standard customers, the first response target is 1 business day for high priority tickets and 3 business days for medium and low priority tickets.

## Escalation
A high priority ticket that stays open for more than 24 hours is escalated automatically to the support team lead. If an Enterprise customer has three or more open high priority tickets at the same time, their account manager is notified and a weekly review call is scheduled.

## Support hours
Standard and Business support runs Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 CET. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support for high priority tickets.
