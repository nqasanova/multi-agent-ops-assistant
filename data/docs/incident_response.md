# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels
SEV1 means a full outage of the ordering platform or a confirmed data breach. SEV2 means a major feature is broken for many customers. SEV3 means a minor issue with a workaround.

## Response steps
For a SEV1 incident, the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 15 minutes and open an incident channel. The incident commander posts a status update every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

For a SEV2 incident, the on-call engineer acknowledges within 30 minutes and posts updates every 2 hours.

## Customer communication
Enterprise customers affected by a SEV1 or SEV2 incident are informed by their account manager within 1 hour. A written postmortem is shared with affected Enterprise customers within 5 business days.

## Data breaches
A confirmed personal data breach must be reported to the data protection officer immediately, so the company can notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by GDPR.
